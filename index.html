about me

I'm finishing my M.S. in Data Science at Michigan State University, following a B.A. in Information Science with a focus area in Human-Centered Technology. Throughout undergrad and grad school, I've had the privilege of working across a pretty wide range of fields, including bioinformatics, academic research, nonprofit work, UX/UI and product development, compliance and safety, and entertainment. Each experience has given me a different perspective on how technology, data, and people intersect, and has shaped the way I approach problems and think about building solutions.

I enjoy building automated systems and workflows in non-technical domains, finding ways to make processes more efficient, and exploring the power of technology and data across different fields to create something useful, intuitive, and easy for a user to navigate and digest.

If I'm not working on data science, I'm usually off macro-dosing on coffee or catching a live show.
