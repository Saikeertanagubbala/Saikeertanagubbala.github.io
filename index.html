about me

hi, i'm keertana. i'm finishing my m.s. in data science at michigan state, after a b.a. in information science. i started out wanting to be a bioinformatician, then fell for big datasets, clean visualizations, and the insights hiding inside them.

now i work where research, automation, and user experience meet: building models and dashboards, and designing the things people actually use. the short version? i bridge the gap between complex data and the people it's meant to help.

off the clock i run on an unreasonable amount of coffee, once spent a whole summer volunteering at a cat cafe, and make a genuinely great tres leches cake. have a look at some of my work below, or just say hi.

currently
  • completing my m.s. in data science at michigan state university
  • building a streamlit dashboard that forecasts maintenance needs for residential buildings across msu
